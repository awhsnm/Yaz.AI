# Guided Inkwell

## Proactive Socratic AI Writing Coach

Guided Inkwell is a web-based writing-support prototype developed by Meiir Aruzhan for the research project:

> Socratic AI as a Writing Scaffold: Effects of Proactive Intervention on Independent Essay Development and Revision Quality in Upper-Secondary Students.

The platform provides a student writing workspace and teacher-facing review tools. Its research-mode prototype is designed to deliver proactive, context-sensitive Socratic questions during argumentative essay writing.

The AI coach is designed to:
- Ask reflective questions about claims, evidence, reasoning, counterarguments, conclusions, and revision.
- Initiate limited prompts after meaningful writing events.
- Preserve student authorship and decision-making.
- Avoid generating essay paragraphs, direct rewrites, citations, sources, or ready-made answers.

## Research status

This repository documents a prototype and pilot-ready research instrument. It does not itself provide evidence that the intervention improves writing quality. Any claims about effectiveness require participant data and a controlled study.
